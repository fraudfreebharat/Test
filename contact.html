Emergency Dental Protocol: Immediate Actions Before You Arrive

1. Knocked-Out Tooth (Avulsion) Handle only by the white crown, never touch the root. Rinse gently with cold water if soiled. Place immediately in a small container of cold whole milk or inside the patient's cheek. Re-implantation within 60 minutes offers the highest success.
2. Severe Throbbing Night Toothache Rinse mouth thoroughly with lukewarm saltwater. Gently use dental floss to dislodge any impacted food debris. Never place aspirin tablets directly against the gum tissue, as this causes severe chemical burns. Call our emergency triage desk.
3. Fractured Tooth or Dislodged Crown Save all tooth fragments in clean water. Cover sharp jagged edges with sugar-free chewing gum or sterile dental wax to protect your tongue and cheeks from laceration while en route to our operatory.
