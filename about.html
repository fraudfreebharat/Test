Clinical Heritage • Founded 2017

Why we replaced assembly-line dentistry with calm, single-specialist precision.

In 2017, after twelve years of handling complex maxillofacial cases and outpatient micro-endodontics across major hospitals, Dr. Mayra Sharma founded this clinic around a clear observation: most people do not avoid the dentist because of tooth neglect—they avoid it because of traumatic past visits and opaque bills.

In conventional outpatient clinics, patients were hurried through 15-minute slots with manual needle injections that caused unnecessary tissue pain, only to find undisclosed fees added to their invoices after the anesthetic took hold.

Mayra Dental Care was designed as the direct antidote. We invested in single-tooth microprocessor anesthesia (Milestone Scientific STA Wand) to eliminate syringe terror, brought in optical 3D scanners to abolish gagging trays, and instituted a strict policy: every patient receives an itemized, fixed fee schedule printed on paper before any clinician touches the tooth.

Practice Charter

Four Non-Negotiable Operating Standards

These principles govern every clinical diagnosis, supply procurement decision, and patient interaction in our practice.

PRINCIPLE 01 Conservation

Tooth Preservation Above Production

We never perform elective crowning on teeth that can be remineralized, sealed, or conservatively restored with biomimetic composite resin. Our doctors receive no commissions or quotas on crowns, veneers, or implants.

PRINCIPLE 02 Sterility

Physical Chemical Indicator Verification

Every surgical kit and examination cassette is sealed in medical-grade barrier pouches and sterilized in our European Class-B pre-vacuum autoclave. The multi-parameter chemical verification strip is unsealed directly at your chairside.

PRINCIPLE 03 Financial

Fixed Itemized Pricing Before Anesthesia

You will never be surprised by add-on charges for dressings, special burs, or post-operative visits. All laboratory material costs, clinician fees, and warranty periods are documented in writing before any procedure commences.

PRINCIPLE 04 Continuity

Dedicated Specialist Continuity

We do not employ rotating junior trainees. The MDS specialist who examines your 3D scan and discusses your diagnosis is the exact clinician who performs your treatment and reviews your post-op healing.
