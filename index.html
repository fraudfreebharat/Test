Clinical Standards Comparison

The Industry Reality vs. The Mayra Standard

We built this practice to systematically address the specific pain points and opaque practices that make patients avoid the dentist.

Common Industry Practice

Conventional Dental Care

Unpredictable Chairside Billing: You receive an initial estimate, only to have unplanned fees for bone grafting, special burs, or dressings added to the bill while your mouth is numb.

Intimidating Syringe Injections: Manual manual plungers cause sudden painful tissue distension, followed by 4 hours of total half-face muscle numbness.

Multiple Root Canal Visits: 3 to 4 appointments required for a single tooth because of outdated manual hand files and lack of electronic apex locators.

Gag-Inducing Impression Putty: Cold, gooey chemical trays that trigger gag reflexes and often warp during postal transit to external labs.

Rotating Junior Doctors: You consult one doctor on your first visit, but a different trainee operates on your crown or root canal the following week.

Our Operational Protocol

The Mayra Dental Care Standard

Printed Itemized Guarantee: Every treatment plan is printed with transparent lab costs, material grades, and written warranty before treatment starts. Zero surprise fees.

Computer-Controlled STA Wand: Microprocessor anesthetic injection delivers pin-point comfort at the single-tooth level without drooping lips or facial paralysis.

Single-Sitting Rotary Endodontics: Over 98% of root canal treatments completed in a single 60-minute session with continuous apical verification.

100% Optical 3D Intraoral Scanning: Sub-millimeter digital files sent directly to CAD/CAM milling units for exact anatomical margins on crowns and aligners.

Continuous Lead Specialist Care: Your entire diagnosis, surgery, and follow-up are handled by your assigned MDS specialist from start to finish.
